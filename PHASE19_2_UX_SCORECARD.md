## 1. 10-Point Forensic Workstation Scoring Dimensions

Each dimension is scored out of **10.0** against strict forensic workstation usability criteria:

1. **Purpose (10/10):** Does the user understand immediately what the workstation view is designed to do?
2. **Case Clarity (10/10):** Is the active case context continuously visible, immutable, and easy to switch?
3. **Source Clarity (10/10):** Is the target storage device/image clearly identified with real metadata?
4. **Action Clarity (10/10):** Is the primary action distinct, unambiguous, and impossible to trigger accidentally?
5. **Workflow Progression (10/10):** Does the workflow guide the investigator through logical stages without skipping safety?
6. **Status Semantics (10/10):** Are operation statuses truthful, distinct, and free of synthetic inflation?
7. **Error Handling (10/10):** Do error states clearly explain WHAT happened, WHY, and WHAT action to take?
8. **Provenance Assurance (10/10):** Are cryptographic hashes, sector offsets, and provenance tags readily available?
9. **Visual Hierarchy (10/10):** Is the screen uncluttered, with high contrast and logical information grouping?
10. **Professionalism (10/10):** Does the interface feel calm, authoritative, forensic, and dependable?
